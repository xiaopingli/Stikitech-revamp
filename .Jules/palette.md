## 2025-06-03 - Form Accessibility Pattern
**Learning:** Adding visible labels to inputs that rely on placeholders significantly improves usability and accessibility, especially for screen readers and when typing long inputs. However, care must be taken to maintain the layout structure (e.g., grid columns) when wrapping inputs in containers.
**Action:** Always check the surrounding layout (like `grid-cols`) before wrapping inputs. Verify that the added labels do not break the design or cause unexpected shifts.
